# 🩺 Bright Path – Health Platform

A modern AI-powered health platform designed to help users monitor their health, manage medical data, and improve their lifestyle through smart insights and automation.

---

## 🚀 Overview

**Bright Path** is a comprehensive health management system that provides:

- Real-time health monitoring
- AI-powered assistance
- Appointment scheduling
- Fitness and nutrition tracking
- Medical records management

The platform delivers a clean dashboard experience with scalable architecture using modern web technologies.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Health Dashboard
- Real-time vitals monitoring:
  - Heart Rate ❤️
  - Blood Oxygen 🫁
  - Blood Pressure 💉
  - Body Temperature 🌡️
- 24-hour trend visualization

---

### 🤖 AI Health Assistant
- Personalized health advice
- Smart recommendations based on user data
- Interactive AI Coach

---

### 🏃 Activity Tracking
- Daily Steps Tracking
- Calories Burned
- Sleep Quality Monitoring
- Goal Progress Indicators

---

### 📅 Appointment Management
- Book appointments easily
- Track upcoming visits
- View appointment status (Confirmed / Pending)

---

### 💊 Medications & Records
- View prescriptions
- Access medical records
- Organized health documents

---

### 🧠 Smart Actions
- Symptom Checker
- Workout Logging
- Quick health insights
